LegadoActivo

A distinction that recognises a living legacy and a belonging that transcends time.

This Distinction does not set a post or impose a function, but recognises a living legacy, an available presence and a belonging that transcends time and circumstance.

As an Active Legacy Mentor, your word will be a reference, your experience a guiding memory and your presence, when needed, a care for the purpose and spirit of this Network.
