Schedule of activities

Three days in Barcelona

Activity 1

Authorities' Cocktail Reception

📍 Barcelona, Spain🗓 Tuesday 26 October 2027

An international meeting bringing together leading figures in mentoring to socialise, discuss and clarify its role as a discipline of human accompaniment, in respectful dialogue with other related practices. A meeting to recognise and honour the professionals who stand out in their communities for their wisdom and generosity.

Activity 2

Conference Day

📍 Barcelona, Spain🗓 Wednesday 27 October 2027

This academic day is offered as a space for critical reflection and constructive dialogue on mentoring in higher education. We aim to explore its transformative potential for the all-round development of students, the training of leaders and the consolidation of learning communities.

Activity 3

Recreational Activity

📍 Barcelona, Spain🗓 Thursday 28 October 2027

A day of leisure among members of the Global Mentoring Network: a tour of the city, networking and a record of the experience.

History of the Congress

Six years, six cities

The International Mentoring Congress is an annual meeting promoted by the Global Mentoring Network. Each edition consolidates mentoring as a mature, ethical and cross-cutting discipline.

  1. IBuenos AiresArgentinaOctober 2022
  2. IIArequipaPeruOctober 2023
  3. IIICDMXMexicoOctober 2024
  4. IVLisbonPortugalOctober 2025
  5. VFloridaUSAOctober 2026
  6. VIBarcelonaSpainOctober 2027
