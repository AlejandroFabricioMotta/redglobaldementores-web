02 · In person

Mentors' Meetings

Local spaces to get to know mentoring in person, in each Ambassador's city.

  • Mentors' Breakfasts
  • After Offices
  • University visits
  • Company visits

They are organised by RGM Ambassadors in their cities and countries. RGM Meetings coordinates them and supports them with the Mentoring Outreach Manual, so that everyone conveys the same message and the same standard.

See scheduled meetings
Under the RGMx brand

Humanising Professions and Trades

RGMx is the brand under which Ambassadors promote mentoring in their city or country, with the same message and the same standard. Among the formats defined in the Manual, mentors' meetings are organised as follows:

University talk45 to 60 minutes
Company workshopHalf a day (2 to 6 hours)
Mentors' Breakfast60 to 90 minutes
Mentors' AfterOffice90 minutes

How an activity is organised, in six steps:

  1. Define
  2. Authorise
  3. Prepare
  4. Carry out
  5. Accompany
  6. Evaluate
If you are an Ambassador and want to organise a meeting, the RGMx licence is requested from Academia Global RGM. Source: Mentoring Outreach Manual, MDM-1010-02, October 2026.

Why we hold meetings

Humanising Professions and Trades

Technology takes on more and more tasks; mentoring develops the judgement, ethics and responsibility of those who practise each profession. Every meeting aims for two outcomes, in this order:

  • Spread the word about mentoring: so that attendees understand what it is, why it matters and how it can transform their profession or trade.
  • Awaken an interest in training and certifying as a mentor: a clear and always voluntary route for anyone who wants to take the next step.
