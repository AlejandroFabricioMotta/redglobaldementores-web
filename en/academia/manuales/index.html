Manual for universities

Master Guide to Mentoring

A special manual for universities.

The Master Guide to Mentoring, published by the RGM Academy, is the international reference work for understanding and practising mentoring with rigour, ethics and professional standards. It brings together the foundations of the discipline with applied tools, methodological models and conceptual frameworks that organise and consolidate mentoring practice.

Academic structure. The work comprises 3 volumes (Level 01, Level 02 and Level 03), designed to accompany the RGM Academy's Academic Programme for Universities at its different levels of training and certification.

The foundational triad

Mentoring as a human path

A journey through history, the present and professional practice.

This triad of Mentoring Guides forms a coherent, foundational body of reading, designed to understand mentoring in its true nature, without confusing it with or diluting it among other disciplines of human accompaniment.

Across its three volumes, the work prepares the reader to understand what mentoring is before trying to practise it, placing it not as a professional technique but as a conscious human experience that comes before any role, certification or method.

It is the central reading base of the Intermediate Mentoring Programme, and has been conceived as a solid, cross-cutting and scalable conceptual architecture, able to provide a foundation for future training, specialisations and new thematic triads.

The functional triad

The art of accompanying consciously

Guides for mentoring in action: the functional triad of conscious mentoring.

This triad is designed for practising mentors who want to keep developing their practice. In human accompaniment, good intentions and an accumulation of tools are not enough; what distinguishes a true professional is the judgement to decide when, how and from which role to intervene.

The Mentoría en Acción series was created to answer this need. More than a collection of techniques or a manual of formulas, it is a training architecture oriented towards the real maturing of the mentor: a comprehensive journey that links judgement, action and reflection, the three inseparable dimensions of conscious practice.

It is the essential prior reading before going deeper into the Niche Manuals: it lays the methodological foundations that every expert mentor must master in order to practise to standard and with excellence.
