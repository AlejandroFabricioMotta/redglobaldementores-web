Specific content

Formative and developmental process · Teacher Mentors

Curriculum content

The aim of this module is to offer Teacher Mentors a set of frameworks, methodologies and pedagogical tools that allow them to accompany training processes with an integral and transformative approach. The goal is for teaching to go beyond the simple transmission of knowledge and become a space of presence, bond and purpose, where teaching is also a way of sowing culture, awareness and legacy.

Throughout this journey, each Teacher Mentor will develop their own Teacher Mentoring Model, integrating the dimensions worked on: vocation, ethics, active listening, the art of questioning, designing learning trajectories and formative assessment. They will be free to go deeper into the aspects that resonate most with their identity and style, without this implying a rigid standardisation of practice.

The purpose is not to standardise the way of teaching or accompanying, but to offer a map of possibilities that each mentor can adapt to the diversity of contexts, styles and student needs. The model they build must have a clear structure, while keeping the flexibility needed to foster autonomy, creativity and critical thinking in learners.

To obtain certification, each Teacher Mentor must present a structured yet flexible Teacher Mentoring Model and pass the theoretical and philosophical exam of the discipline, thus demonstrating their ability to turn the classroom into a space for human growth and for building the future.

Reading

We invite you to read the “Formative and Developmental Process”

Formative and developmental process · Teacher Mentors

In this document you will find a transformative proposal for education: the Formative and Developmental Process for Teacher Mentors. It is not a conventional course but an experiential journey in which you will discover how to go from being a transmitter of content to becoming an architect of learning and a catalyst of human development.

Through ten structured dialogues, it addresses topics such as the teaching vocation, active listening, the art of questioning, the design of learning trajectories and meaningful assessment. Each phase combines theory, practice, reflection and the construction of a personal logbook, inviting you to write your own Teacher Mentor Book.

Reading this material is an invitation to rethink teaching with purpose, ethics and legacy. We encourage you to immerse yourself in its pages and let yourself be inspired to transform your educational practice.

First Meeting

Inhabiting the call to be a Teacher Mentor

Differences between instruction, education and development. Introduction to the three roles of the Teacher Mentor. Vocation logbook. Start of reading the Master Guide.

Connection to the Mentoring Master Guide: general introduction to the guide and definition of the mentor. Start of personal study of Chapters 1 and 2 of the manual.

Second Meeting

From function to meaning

Didactic triad: problem, object, objective. The ethical role of the teacher as a designer of experiences. Practices of listening and presence.

GMM connection: guided study of Chapter 3: Ethical and philosophical principles of the mentor. Written reflection.

Third Meeting

From knowing to being

Differences between teaching, training, accompanying and mentoring. Levels of learning: reproductive, productive, creative. Training experiences with legacy.

GMM connection: reading and application of Chapter 4: The mentor as a catalyst of human development.

Fourth Meeting

Listening as the basis of meaningful learning

Foundations of meaningful learning. The mediating role of the teacher. Listening techniques and constructive feed-forward.

GMM connection: Chapter 6: Key skills of the mentor. Focus on listening, empathy and emotional regulation.

Fifth Meeting

Transforming through inquiry

The question as a trigger of thought. Table of question levels. Scaffolding. Didactics of inquiry.

GMM connection: Chapter 7: Designing your personal mentoring method. Practical application.

Sixth Meeting

Designing trajectories

Design structure: content – method – form. Cooperative learning strategies. Learning trajectories and autonomy.

GMM connection: Chapters 8 and 9: Session models, structured processes and adaptation to contexts.

Seventh Meeting

Bond and purpose

Pedagogical bond. Classroom climate. Pedagogical leadership and intervention styles.

GMM connection: Chapter 10: Mentoring in action. Chapter 11: Purpose and transformation.

Eighth Meeting

Assessment with meaning

Types of assessment. Assessment as mediation. Student portfolio and mentor logbook.

GMM connection: Chapter 12: Building a differentiating value proposition.

Ninth Meeting

Legacy and transformation

Institutional culture, learning community. Educational life projects. Pedagogical legacy and continuous mentoring.

GMM connection: Chapters 13 and 14: Legacy, impact and mentoring in the digital age.

Tenth Meeting

Final integration

Delivery of logbooks, statement of purpose, symbolic circle. Final integration of what has been learned.

GMM connection: Chapter 15: Impact assessment and sustainability. Overall review of learning across the whole guide.

Your path

Your path to certification as an Expert Mentor in Continuing Education

You have completed the 10 training dialogues with Dr. Nelson de J. Rueda Restrepo. Only one step remains to turn that journey into your official certification.

What does the certification consist of?
There is no exam and no additional stages. You obtain your certification by submitting the Logbook you built throughout the 10 Meetings — your own Teacher Mentor Book — where your Teacher Mentoring Model was recorded: your vocation, your ethics, the way you listen, ask questions and accompany learning processes.

That Logbook is the evidence of your transformation: from a transmitter of content to an Expert Mentor in Continuing Education, able to accompany training processes with purpose, presence and legacy.

Final steps to get certified
If you have already completed your closing interview:

  • Pay your certification enrolment fee (if you have not yet; the amount depends on whether you already hold the “Expert Mentor” certification).
  • Send your Logbook by email to Academia@RGMentores.org / info@RGMentores.org.
  • Wait up to 30 days to receive your credentials.

Need help? Your Training Centre is there to support you. You can also write to us on WhatsApp at +54 9 341 5421624: our team will be glad to guide you through this last stretch. Your professional journey deserves it. Get certified today!
