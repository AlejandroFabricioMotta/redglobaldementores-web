120 h

Intensive Mentoring Programme

Essential asynchronous material for acquiring core knowledge of mentoring at your own pace. Previous experience in leading sessions with clients (mentoring, coaching, advisory, consulting or another support discipline) is required. Once you complete it you can access the Certification Platform, pay the registration fee and take the assessment to obtain certification as an Expert Mentor.

Leads to your certification as an “Expert Mentor”

Available in Spanish, English and Portuguese.

Enter →

180 h

Intermediate Mentoring Programme

University diploma programme · 180 hours · 1 academic year. For people with no previous experience, professionals who want to systematise their early practice and those seeking a diploma with an intermediate workload. It draws on the triad “Mentoring as a Human Path” (La Mentoría como Camino Humano). It includes training exercises; it does not by itself qualify you to practise independently as a professional.

Contact a Training Centre: ask for your access code

Intensive format: 6 months. Regular format: 9 to 12 months.

Enter →

360 / 600 h

Comprehensive Mentoring Programme

Contact a university to take this training. Once you have access you will find videos, manuals and exercises that will prepare you for the “Expert Mentor” certification. Duration: 360 h (1 academic year) or 600 h (2 years). Levels: Practitioner Mentor (Master Guide, Volume I), Professional Mentor (Volume II) and Expert Mentor (Volume III).

Ask your university for the access code

Leads to “Expert Mentor” or to “Expert Mentor in one of the niches”.
