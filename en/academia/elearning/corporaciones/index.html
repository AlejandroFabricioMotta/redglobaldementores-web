Expert Mentor in Corporate Management and Leadership

Tools, strategies and methodologies to empower leaders and transform organisations.

Specialty training for those who are already «Expert Mentors». «Leadership is not imposed; it is inspired. The mentor guides, the leader transforms.»

Who it is for

Structuring what you already know

People who enter this training have already obtained their «Expert Mentor» certification. That is why what you will find here is a very specific specialty, aimed at a market niche.

You are not here to learn how to be a mentor, but to structure what you already know. Academia Global developed the «Niche Manuals» to show the Expert Mentor how to approach some of the problems specific to that niche.

Niche Manual

Your reference manual

Manual of the Expert Mentor in Corporate Management and Leadership

A model for turning experience, knowledge and leadership into intellectual capital. The manual supports those who already have a track record in the world of organisations and need to learn to intervene where human experience, strategic knowledge, leadership and corporate value meet: reading where knowledge is concentrated, where it is lost and where it does not circulate, and helping a person, a team or an organisation see better, think better, decide better and leave a capability installed.

Epilogue and bibliography

The pathway

Four steps to your monograph

  • 1. Read the manual as a reference
    Go through it with the eyes of someone who already practises mentoring: the manual does not teach you the craft, it shows you how this niche is approached.
  • 2. Choose a problem you have lived through
    Think of a real process in which you accompanied a mentee, with your mentoring model, through a situation specific to this niche.
  • 3. Develop your monograph
    Describe the approach: the dynamics, the tools and all the resources needed for mentoring practice.
  • 4. Submit and get certified
    Send your final monograph to Academia Global by email; its approval is your certification process.
Your deliverable

The monograph

The certification candidate uses the niche manual as a reference and develops a monograph to deliver to Academia Global.

In it, they describe the approach to a problem they have lived through with their mentoring model in order to accompany a mentee through it, developing the dynamics, tools and all the resources needed for mentoring practice.
