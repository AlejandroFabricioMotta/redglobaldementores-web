Institutional presentation of Academia Global RGM

Who we are

A leading institution

Since its creation in 2014, the RGM Academy has built an international ecosystem dedicated to professionalising mentoring as a human, strategic and scientifically grounded discipline. Born as part of the development of the Global Mentoring Network, the Academy has grown into a leading institution for the training, research and certification of professional mentors across many regions and languages.

Academic mission

Role in the global ecosystem

The RGM Academy's mission is to unify, structure and raise the training standard of mentoring, offering academic programmes, technical guides, specialised manuals and assessment systems that guarantee the quality, consistency and traceability of the training process in any country in the world.

Through an integrating vision, the Academy sets international criteria, promotes professional ethics and defines pedagogical standards that serve as the basis for its associated Training Centres and the smaller academies that operate worldwide under its supervision.

Academic leadership

Excellence, rigour and experience

The Academy is led by Dr. Nelson de J. Rueda, a continental reference in mentoring, human resource management, applied neuroscience, executive coaching and the development of human potential. His academic career, research work and ability to link theoretical foundations with applied methodologies have established him as one of the leading architects of contemporary mentoring.

His leadership guarantees:

  • Epistemological rigour in the definition of the training models.
  • Constant updating in neuroscience, behavioural science and deep-learning methodologies.
  • Ethical soundness in the certification criteria.
  • Academic curation of texts, guides and study plans.
Academic programmes and functional guides

Structured, progressive training

Programmes built on more than a decade of research.

Comprehensive Mentoring Programme

Master Guide to Mentoring. A three-level pathway that brings together philosophical, historical and epistemological foundations; mentor–mentee dynamics from a humanistic, systemic and strategic perspective; and supervised professional practice with criteria of competence, ethics and global standards.

Specialisation Programmes (Niche Mentoring)

Created and endorsed by the Academy to train Expert Mentors in corporations, education, entrepreneurship, family and society, sport, culture and other fields. They are developed in partnership with recognised professionals, under the Academy's methodological supervision and validation.

Intensive Programme

Essential Guide to Mentoring. Certification for professionals from many disciplines who need rigorous, clear and practical training. The guide, of more than 100 pages, summarises the theoretical and technical body of knowledge essential for professional practice.

Assessment and certification

Ethical assessment systems

One of the Academy's distinguishing strengths is the use of evidence-based assessment systems. The process ensures that certification reflects real competence, ethics and effective ability to accompany others.

  • Formative, competence-based assessments.
  • Audiovisual evidence of professional practice.
  • Writing applied value propositions.
  • Closing interviews with certified Teaching Mentors.

Global supervision

Associated academies

The RGM Academy does not just train mentors: it manages, trains and audits smaller academies in different countries, ensuring that every institution under its umbrella meets the defined academic, ethical and pedagogical standards.

  • International scalability.
  • Unified criteria.
  • Quality control.
  • Recognition of certifications.
  • Expansion in multiple languages.
Technology platform

Multilingual reach

The Academy integrates a technology system that makes it possible to scale training globally in three main languages: Spanish, Portuguese and English. The technology ensures that the academic programmes reach any country with quality, precision and consistency.

  • Latest-generation virtual classrooms.
  • Standardised multilingual materials.
  • Automated academic tracking.
  • Audiovisual resources and downloadable guides.
  • API for intelligent interaction with study and self-assessment tools.
Track record 2014 – 2025

Maturity and global reach

Over more than a decade, the RGM Academy has gone through a gradual process of consolidation that includes:

  • Empirical validation with thousands of trained mentors.
  • Integration with highly prestigious professional communities.
  • Construction of its own theoretical framework.
  • Partnerships with educational institutions and professional networks on several continents.
  • Development of original study materials and specialised manuals.

Today it is positioned as the leading academy unifying the concepts and standards of mentoring, with growth prospects that will establish it as the global academic reference in the training and certification of expert mentors.

University standard

An academy created for the university standard

The RGM Academy has an institutional, academic and technological structure aligned with the criteria that elite universities require in order to endorse or integrate training programmes. Its academic leadership, methodological maturity and international expansion make it an ideal partner for generating:

  • Joint programmes.
  • Certifications with dual endorsement.
  • Applied research.
  • Specialised university outreach in mentoring.

The RGM Academy aspires, with the right backing, to become the world's most prestigious institution for training and certification as an Expert Mentor, connecting science, professional practice, technology and ethics in a single model of excellence.
